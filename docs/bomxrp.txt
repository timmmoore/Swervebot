3.3V split 4 ways to 4 swerve modules to motor encoder
5V split 4 ways to 4 swerve modules to through-bore SRX
Gnd split 4 (12) ways to encoder, through-core and motor controller

Each swerve module - 12pin connectors
	power - 11.V, Gnd
	motor controller - Gnd, Pwm1 (32, 10, 20, 34), Pwm2 (33, 11, 21, 35)
	encoder - 3.3V, Gnd, A (24, 2, 22, 30), B (25, 3, 23, 31)
	Through-bore - 5V, Gnd, Pwm-in (43, 42, 41, 40)
